How It Works

From Wall to Wow — in 3 Steps

Our certified installation crew handles every panel, sheet and joint so you don't have to worry about a thing.

Step 01

Surface Preparation

Panels are secured to the wall using a specialised heat-bonding adhesive paste for a firm base layer.

Step 02

Adhesive Application

Silicone adhesive is loaded into a caulking gun and applied in careful lines roughly every 10 inches.

Step 03

Final Placement

The panel is pressed firmly into place until the adhesive bonds completely — leaving a seamless finish.
